Facebook & Instagram
Business Setup Guide

Connect your Facebook Business Page to DigiM app and start publishing AI-generated campaigns in minutes.

✅ Prerequisites BEFORE YOU BEGIN
🏢 Facebook Business Page You must be an Admin of the Page. Personal profiles are not supported.
👤 Facebook Account The account must have Admin access to the Business Page.
📸 Instagram (optional) Must be a Business or Creator account — Personal accounts are not supported by Meta's API.
🔗 Instagram linked to Page Link Instagram to your Facebook Page via Meta Business Suite first.
⚠️ Do not use a Personal Instagram account. Switch to a Business or Creator profile first: Instagram → Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account.
📋 Step-by-Step Setup
1
Navigate to Platform Settings

Open DigiM app in your browser. Click the Integrations option in the left sidebar (gear icon).

2
Connect Your Facebook Page

Locate the Facebook Page card (blue f icon) and click Connect. A Facebook login popup will appear — log in with the account that administers your Business Page.

ℹ️ If you see "Facebook SDK is still loading", wait 2–3 seconds and try again.

Grant all requested permissions:

pages_show_listSee which Pages you manage
pages_read_engagementRead Page content
pages_manage_postsPublish posts on your behalf
instagram_basicRead your linked Instagram
instagram_content_publishPublish photos to Instagram
⚠️ Grant all permissions. Skipping any will cause publishing to fail silently.
3
Select Your Business Page

After granting permissions, a "Select Facebook Page" dropdown appears inside DigiM app. Choose your Page from the list and click Confirm.

💡 DigiM app automatically upgrades your short-lived login token to a non-expiring Page access token — you won't need to reconnect.
4
Connect Instagram (Optional)

Once your Facebook Page is connected, the Instagram Business card becomes active. Click Connect — no second login needed.

ℹ️ DigiM app automatically finds the Instagram Business account linked to your Facebook Page. No extra login required.

Both cards will show a green ✅ checkmark when successfully connected.

5
Publish Your First Campaign

Go to Campaigns → generate your AI content or write your own post → click "Publish to Facebook". If Instagram is connected, a toggle appears to publish to both platforms simultaneously.

💡 Instagram posts require an image — text-only posts are not supported by the Instagram API.
🔧 Troubleshooting
❌ "No Facebook Pages found"
Your Facebook account has no Pages. Create one at facebook.com/pages/create, or ask your Page admin to add you as an Admin (not just Editor).
❌ Instagram "Connect" button stays grayed out
Your Instagram account is not linked to your Facebook Page, or it's a Personal account. Open Meta Business Suite → Settings → Instagram Accounts → click Add to link it.
❌ Instagram publish fails with "media container" error
Instagram requires the image URL to be publicly accessible. Localhost or private storage URLs will fail. Use a CDN or cloud storage link.
❌ Post published but doesn't appear on the Page
Check your Facebook Page's Publishing Tools → Scheduled Posts. The post may be pending review or scheduled for a later time.
📎 Quick Checklist
  • I have a Facebook Business Page and I am an Admin of it
  • I opened DigiM app and navigated to Integrations
  • I clicked Connect and granted all permissions in the Facebook popup
  • I selected my Business Page from the dropdown and clicked Confirm
  • (Optional) My Instagram is a Business/Creator account linked to my Facebook Page
  • (Optional) The Instagram card shows ✅ Connected
  • I published a test campaign and confirmed it appeared on my Page
