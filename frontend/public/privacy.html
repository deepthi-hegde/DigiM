Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 03, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DigiM app ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our web application, specifically in relation to our Meta (Facebook and Instagram) integration.

1. Information We Collect

To provide AI-managed campaign publishing and marketing performance analytics, we collect the following data upon your authorization through Meta's OAuth system:

  • Access Tokens: Temporary and non-expiring Page and User access tokens required to publish content and query reach insights.
  • Page & Account Information: Your Facebook Page name, Page ID, linked Instagram Business account ID, and linked profile details.
  • Campaign Assets: Text captions and visual assets uploaded or generated by you to be shared on your connected social media channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected permissions solely to support core application functions on your behalf:

  • To fetch performance insights (reach, impressions, follower growth) and display them in your analytics dashboard.
  • To publish AI-generated promotional text, images, or videos directly to your authorized Facebook Page or Instagram Business Profile.

We do not sell, distribute, or share your Meta account data, tokens, or profile insights with any third-party services or advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Authorized access tokens are stored securely in our protected database to prevent unauthorized access. The credentials are encrypted in transit and at rest.

4. Revoking Permissions & Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your connected accounts at all times:

  • Revoke Access: You can disconnect your Meta accounts directly inside the DigiM "Integrations" settings panel, or revoke the "DigiM" app permissions globally through your Facebook Account settings under "Business Integrations".
  • Request Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your account metadata, access tokens, and stored campaign analytics from our database, please contact our support team or trigger deletion via your settings panel. All records will be wiped immediately upon request.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
