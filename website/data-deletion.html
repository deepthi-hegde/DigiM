User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 | Last Updated: November 9, 2026

According to the Facebook Platform rules and General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR / Indian DPDP), users have the right to request the deletion of their personal data collected through applications connected to Facebook.

If you have connected your Facebook Account or Facebook Pages to Sri Krishna Associates or the DigiM marketing application (App ID: 3531281670380966) and wish to remove your data, please follow the instructions below:

Method 1: Remove App Access via Facebook Settings

  1. Go to your Facebook profile's Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Scroll down and click on Apps and Websites in the left menu.
  3. Locate Business (or DigiM) in the list of active connected apps.
  4. Click the Remove button next to the app name.
  5. Select the checkbox if you want Facebook to delete your posts and content published through the app, then confirm by clicking Remove.

Method 2: Request Complete Data Purge from Our Servers

If you would like us to permanently delete all access tokens, Facebook Page identifiers, and stored marketing campaign metadata associated with your account from our database:

Send an email to our official data protection administrator:

  • Subject Line: User Data Deletion Request - Facebook App
  • Required Information: Include your Facebook Page Name or your registered business email so we can locate your record.

Upon receiving your request, our team will permanently erase your access credentials and data records within 48 hours and send you a confirmation email.
